How to find a folder ID
  1. Open Google Drive in your browser
  2. Create a folder for the category (e.g. "Treasury Devices") or open an existing one
  3. Look at the browser URL bar — it ends with a long string of letters and numbers
  4. Copy everything after folders/ — that's the folder ID
  5. Paste it into the field above and click Save
